How Will reviews a cut: Y/N/Change

Every cut or clip gets stacked as yes/no/change calls.

  • YES Keep as-is.
  • NO Cut it, put it away. FFRF wording: "put in bad options folder".
  • CHANGE Keep it with a specific trim or edit instruction, for example: "start 30 seconds earlier", "end it 30 seconds later", "make it go a minute longer", "add 2 minutes after".
FFRF version
Numbered clips with a verdict each (really good / good / meh / not great) plus the trim direction plus timestamps.
Riverside version
One stacked yes/no list per judgment call (intro take, CTA splice, CTA take choice, 16:9 vs 9:16, etc).
